# Privacy Policy — Sovereign-Alpha-Matrix (@sovereign_alphmatrixbot)

**Last Updated:** November 2026

## 1. Data We Collect
Sovereign-Alpha-Matrix only processes the minimum Telegram metadata required to respond to your commands (`/start`, `/status`, `/spread`) and deliver threshold alerts:
- Your numeric Telegram **Chat ID** and **User ID**
- Command text sent directly to the bot

## 2. How Data Is Used
- Used strictly in real time to return ZAR cryptocurrency arbitrage metrics and automated spread alerts.
- We do **not** sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

## 3. Data Retention & Deletion
- Chat IDs authorized for automated alerts are stored securely in local environment configuration.
- You may stop receiving messages at any time by blocking the bot in Telegram or requesting removal.

## 4. Jurisdiction & Contact
Operated from South Africa in alignment with POPIA principles. For data inquiries or deletion requests, contact the bot administrator via Telegram.
